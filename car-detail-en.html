How to read a vehicle profile

Vehicle specifications vary by model year, trim, and market. Use this guide to verify the right source before buying or comparing a vehicle.

Essential checks

  • Identify the make, model, model year, trim, and market.
  • Use the manufacturer's page or owner manual for specifications and equipment.
  • Match safety results to the exact model year and equipment.
  • Check maintenance history, condition, and local-dealer pricing.

Reliable sources

  • Euro NCAP
  • IIHS
  • NHTSA
