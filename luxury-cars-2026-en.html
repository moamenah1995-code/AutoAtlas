How to read this comparison

Test front and rear seats on rough and highway roads, operate driver-assistance features, and price tires, brakes and insurance. Specifications describe a particular trim and market; they do not prove quality or resale value.

Figures are tied to the stated market, trim and test cycle. Manufacturer estimates are not independent measurements; real range or consumption changes with weather, speed, load and tires. Prices and warranty claims from the submitted draft were omitted because they lacked a verifiable local source and like-for-like basis.
