Sports cars for 2026: performance is more than an acceleration time

Power and acceleration alone do not define a sports car. Tires, brakes, cooling, mass and repeatable performance over multiple laps determine track capability.

How to read this comparison

The chart shows manufacturer-stated peak output for selected U.S. versions; it does not compare lap time, handling or braking. Acceleration varies with transmission, tires, fuel and test conditions. High-speed testing belongs on a closed course.

Figures are tied to the stated market, trim and test cycle. Manufacturer estimates are not independent measurements; real range or consumption changes with weather, speed, load and tires. Prices and warranty claims from the submitted draft were omitted because they lacked a verifiable local source and like-for-like basis.
