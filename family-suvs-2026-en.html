Best family SUVs for 2026? Compare space, efficiency and use

A seven-seat badge does not guarantee useful third-row space. Access, luggage capacity with all seats in use, and measured fuel or electric use matter more than a broad marketing claim.

How to read this comparison

Start with passenger count, child seats, cargo and road type. A conventional hybrid does not plug in; a plug-in hybrid needs regular charging to deliver electric driving; a battery EV needs a realistic charging plan and route fit.

Figures are tied to the stated market, trim and test cycle. Manufacturer estimates are not independent measurements; real range or consumption changes with weather, speed, load and tires. Prices and warranty claims from the submitted draft were omitted because they lacked a verifiable local source and like-for-like basis.
