About AutoAtlas

We are a Jordan-based technical and engineering team focused on renewable energy engineering and automotive technology, including vehicle systems and maintenance. We aim to share knowledge and build public awareness of vehicles and sustainable mobility. We believe accurate, well-sourced information helps people make better decisions, reduce emissions, and support efficient, intelligent mobility solutions. AutoAtlas is a non-commercial educational knowledge platform, and we aspire to make it a comprehensive, trustworthy reference for the automotive world.

What we provide

Concise cards, clear comparisons and articles that explain data limits instead of inventing precision.

How we check

Claims are tied to manufacturer or official-body sources, with market, year and trim differences explained.

What we are not

We are not a dealer, price authority or substitute for a local inspection before purchase.
