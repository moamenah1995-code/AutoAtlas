Introduction

This policy explains how AutoAtlas handles information and data in line with good practice and relevant Google requirements.

Information we collect

  • We do not require visitors to create an account.
  • We do not request names, email addresses, or phone numbers for normal browsing.
  • Non-personal technical data, such as browser and device type, may be collected automatically to improve performance.

Cookies

Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure performance and improve the visitor experience. Visitors can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings.

Advertising and Google AdSense

When advertising is enabled on AutoAtlas, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. Ads may be based on a visitor's previous visits to this site or to other sites, in accordance with Google's settings and policies.

Visitors can manage ad personalisation through Google's My Ad Center and learn about how Google uses data when visitors use partner sites or apps. Where required by law, visitors will be asked to make choices about consent for cookies.

How information is used

  • To improve content and user experience.
  • To analyze overall site performance statistically and without identifying visitors.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements.
